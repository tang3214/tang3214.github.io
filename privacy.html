Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026
Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FengMang Video Publisher (蜂芒视频发布助手, the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App and connect it to your TikTok account. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

The App is a desktop tool that helps you generate short product videos with AI and publish them to your own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. You authorize the App through TikTok Login Kit. The App is designed to keep your data on your own computer:

  • Access tokens are stored locally on your device only.
  • The App only uploads videos that you explicitly select and confirm.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information we collect from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account and grant permission, the App may receive the following information from TikTok, limited to the scopes you approve:

DataScopePurpose
Basic profile information (such as open ID / union ID, display name, and avatar image) user.info.basic To identify the connected account and show it in the App so you can confirm where videos will be published.
OAuth access token, refresh token, token expiry, and granted scopes Login Kit To make authorized API requests to TikTok on your behalf when you choose to upload or publish a video.
Upload and publishing status information returned by TikTok (such as publish ID and status) video.upload, video.publish To show you whether your upload or post succeeded.
Creator posting options returned by TikTok (such as available privacy levels and interaction settings) video.publish To let you choose valid settings before posting.

We do not receive or store your TikTok password. We do not access your followers, messages, or other TikTok content beyond what is listed above.

3. Information you provide in the App

  • Content materials: product images, text descriptions, prompts, captions, and other materials you provide to create videos.
  • Generated videos: the videos created in the App and the captions and settings you choose for them.
  • App settings: your preferences and configuration, stored locally.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account and display which account is connected;
  • Upload and publish videos that you explicitly select, with the caption and settings you choose;
  • Show you upload and publishing status;
  • Generate video content from the materials you provide; and
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's features. We do not post, comment, like, follow, or take any other action on your TikTok account without your explicit instruction.

5. Storage and security

  • Access tokens, refresh tokens, and basic profile information are stored locally on your own computer only. They are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by us.
  • Videos and content materials are stored locally on your computer. A video is transmitted to TikTok only when you choose to upload or publish it.
  • Communication with TikTok's APIs takes place over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • You are responsible for keeping your computer and user account secure. Anyone with access to your computer may be able to use the App with your connected TikTok account.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

  • Tokens and profile information are kept locally until you disconnect your TikTok account in the App, the tokens expire or are revoked, or you uninstall the App and delete its local data.
  • Generated videos and materials remain on your computer until you delete them.
  • Videos you publish to TikTok are governed by TikTok's own policies; you can manage or delete them in TikTok.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

  • Disconnect: you can disconnect your TikTok account in the App at any time, which deletes locally stored tokens and profile information.
  • Revoke access: you can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions, or the equivalent menu in your TikTok version).
  • Delete local data: you can delete the App's local data folder or uninstall the App.
  • Requests: to ask a question or request access to or deletion of any data related to you, contact us at tangjingcheng1997@outlook.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the period required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live (for example, Mexico, the European Economic Area, or certain U.S. states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. You may exercise these rights by contacting us.

8. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is shared only as follows:

  • TikTok: the videos, captions, and settings you choose to publish are sent to TikTok through its official APIs. TikTok's handling of this data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • AI service providers: if you use AI video generation features, the content materials you provide (such as product images, descriptions, and prompts) may be processed by AI service providers solely to generate your content. Your TikTok access tokens and TikTok profile data are not shared with these providers.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children. It is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, and who meet TikTok's own age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law). If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

The App is used on your own computer, and most data stays on your device. Data you send to TikTok or to AI service providers may be processed in other countries according to those providers' policies.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the App or on this website. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: tangjingcheng1997@outlook.com
